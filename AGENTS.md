# Tian

Tian (天, _tiān_: day, sky; 天天向上, a little further every day) is a set of games for learning Mandarin (simplified characters), in English, at https://tian.ymse.land. It is one of the sites of Ymseland (see the ymseland skill, and https://ymse.land/oversikt). It was `/chinese` in the homepage repo until October 2026.

## The repo

- Astro with one React island (`client:only`), Tailwind v4, built to static files.
- The game is `src/app/`; paths below without a folder are in it. The page is `src/pages/index.astro`; `/admin` lists who has signed in.
- Convex (project `tian`) keeps the word bank on the account (`convex/bank.ts`), who has signed in (`convex/access.ts`) and the daily reminders (`convex/reminders.ts`, below): anyone can sign in with Google and keep their bank on the account. Each new account is sent to Gorm's inbox at ymse.land/admin/innboks, which pushes it to the phone, with the sender's token in `YMSELAND_NOTIFY_TOKEN` on production's Convex (without it, nothing is sent); `/admin` lists them all.
- Hosting: the Cloudflare Worker `tian`, built by Workers Builds on push to `main` (other branches get preview URLs). The files are static assets; `worker/index.ts` runs only for `/api/auth/*`, passing Google's sign-in on to Convex so that Google's page names tian.ymse.land (Convex's `CUSTOM_AUTH_SITE_URL`), and for `/podcast/*`, serving the podcast from the R2 bucket `tian-podcast` (below). Headers are in `public/_headers`, and old URLs are redirected in `public/_redirects`.
- Google sign-in has a Cloud project, client and brand of its own: project «Tian» (`tian-511918`, owned by gormroedder@gmail.com), client «Tian», with `https://tian.ymse.land/api/auth/callback/google` as its only redirect URI, so Google's page says «Tian». The brand is verified and the app published (External), with `/about` as its home page, `/privacy` as its privacy policy and `ymse.land` as its authorized domain (verified in Google Search Console by a TXT record in the zone, which must stay). Keep `/privacy` in step with `convex/schema.ts`. Changing the app's name, home page or privacy link means verifying the brand again, and a logo means a full app review. Production's Convex is `valuable-akita-141`, with `SITE_URL` and `CUSTOM_AUTH_SITE_URL` https://tian.ymse.land; development's (`dutiful-hummingbird-800`) has no Google sign-in.

Commands:

## The game

`index.tsx` is the shell: one game per hash (`/#tones`), each loaded lazily from `games/`, and the home screen (`home.tsx`) without one. `mend.tsx` catches what breaks, the whole page and each game apart, so the page never goes blank: a game's code gone after a deploy (the installed app stays open for days) reloads the page once, and anything else shows a way to load again. To add a game, add a file there and a line to `GAMES` in `registry.tsx`; `GROUPS` in `home.tsx` puts it under a heading (or the game names its own `group`; one in neither shows under More). The page installs as an app of its own (`public/manifest.webmanifest`, icons in `public/app/`, drawn from the seal beside the home screen's title by `node scripts/icons.mjs`).

To look at a change without a dev server, build, serve `dist/` and take screenshots with `scripts/shot.mjs` (puppeteer; `--learner` seeds a bank with some words met and known, `--mobile` for a phone, `--click "<text>"` presses a button first, `--wait <ms>` lets animations settle). Some pages read the query: `/?time=21:30` (or `?date=2027-02-06&time=20:00`) shows the home screen's scene at that hour, `?story=pangu&line=3#stories` opens a story at a line, `?cast#stories` lays out every puppet, `?stall=fan&mood=1#market` starts a market day at a stall, in one of its three moods, and `?meet=dragon#bridge` begins the starship's voyage with that encounter. `?mute` silences the page for testing: speech takes its time but is not heard, and there are no sound effects or music.

## The word bank

Every game reads and writes one bank (`lib/bank.ts`), so a word learnt in one is known in all. A word is three links, learnt apart: hanzi and sound (`hs`: saying the character, knowing it by ear), hanzi and meaning (`hm`: reading it) and sound and meaning (`sm`: understanding it said, or in pinyin), shown as 字音, 字义 and 音义. Writing is not one: Gorm wants to read, not write, so Brush counts toward nothing and Today does not pick it. Each word met has a card with a memory per link in the manner of FSRS: `s` is the stability in days, recall is `1 / (1 + t / 9s)`, a right answer multiplies `s` by more the more it had faded (crammed answers count little, spaced ones a lot), a wrong one cuts it, and a real slip on a link to the character brings a known word back to pinyin. A word is as faded as its weakest link. Games weigh what an answer says and name what it tested (`record(id, right, weight, how)`): typing pinyin 1, picking from four about 0.6, hints that a word was looked up 0.3; `how` is a link (typing pinyin, rain and tones are `hs`, anything understood by ear `sm`) or `'read'` for text shown as the page shows it, which is `hm` for a word in hanzi alone and `sm` for one with pinyin over it. Each link is asked one way or the other (`Dir` in `lib/bank.ts`: `h>s` the pinyin typed, `s>h` the character heard picked, `h>m` and `m>h`, `s>m` the meaning heard, `m>s` the pinyin for a meaning picked), and a question that knows its way records it as `how`; the card then counts how often each way is missed (`d`, misses and answers fading by a quarter with each answer, merged by the later), without showing it. Review and Today ask each word about its weakest link (`questionsFor` in `quiz.tsx`), one of its ways: its meaning from the character alone or the character for a meaning (`hm`), the character heard, or its pinyin typed once that link lasts (`hs`), its meaning heard, or its pinyin for a meaning (`sm`). Each way has a share (typing two to one once it is allowed, hearing two to one), multiplied by how often it is missed, by the word or, while the word has few answers that way, by the player over all words, so the way missed more is asked more. Cards saved before the links (one `s` per word) are read as having that memory in each, and merged per link, the one answered last. The wrong answers of a question of four (`lib/choices.ts`) must not give it away by their shape: by ear and for picking the character they have as many syllables as the right one (from the whole vocabulary if the words met have too few), and by ear they share syllables or tones with it, and its characters in the same place (hearing bù first, 不客气 is not the only choice that begins with 不), from real words never taught (`data/decoys.ts`) where the vocabulary has too few; a line heard is offered beside meanings of lines about as long, and a number beside numbers said in as many syllables.

A word's card decides how it is shown everywhere (`Mixed`, `Sprinkled` and `Ruby` in `ui.tsx`), always in hanzi, as Hola always shows Spanish, with the help its stage needs (`stage` in `lib/bank.ts`, 0–3, `stageOf` in the store; `Helped` in `ui.tsx` draws it):

- not met (0) and learning (1): pinyin above and English under it, in Chinese word order ("I want two (item) steamed bun"); not met in the brown of an old gloss, learning in jade. Mixed text gives every word of a line a row under it when any word has English, so the line stays even;
- its meaning held (2: `hm` or `sm` lasting 1.5 days or more, not faded): pinyin above, the English gone;
- known (3: both links to the character lasting 1.5 days or more, not faded): hanzi alone.

A word whose character brings back its sound before its meaning keeps pinyin and English until its meaning holds. `level` (0–2) is the same with the two middle stages as one, Learning, which is what the games ask.

`lib/store.tsx` holds the bank and the settings as React state, in `localStorage` (`tian:bank:v1`, `tian:settings:v1`). Signed in, the bank is also kept on the account (`convex/bank.ts`, as the page's own JSON) and merged on sign-in: per word the card seen last, per score, day, scene and garden thing the highest, per seal the first day it was earned, and of two Today sessions the later day's, or the one further on.

## The daily habit

- Today (`lib/today.ts`, `games/today.tsx`): a session of about ten minutes, made once a day from the bank and kept in it (`bank.path`), so it can be taken up again on any device: fading words, a few new ones, a trip scene (two days in three) or else a second round of the words slipping most (`shore`, 巩固 Shore up: the next most faded after the review's, each asked about its weakest link), some listening, then a summary. Games are no longer a step: a game played on its own page has no end to lead back from, and the listening after it was missed; the trip's end and a part of First steps lead back to Today with a button, and the trip can be put off for words to shore up. The steps played in Today keep each answer as it is given (`got` on the step: a word reviewed, a new word met and asked its meaning and by ear, a line heard), so a step can be left anywhere and goes on from there; it is done with its last answer. For a beginner the session is the day of First steps instead (below). It is the home screen's main card; `daily/TodayDock.tsx` leads back to it from a step played on its own page.
- Seals (`data/seals.ts`, `lib/achievements.ts`, `games/seals.tsx`): achievements as red seal stamps, each carved with a character it teaches. What earns one is read from the bank alone, so it is earned in whichever game does it; `daily/SealToaster.tsx` stamps new ones anywhere on the page.
- The garden (`data/garden.ts`, `lib/garden.ts`, `garden/`, `games/garden.tsx`): a courtyard where each lesson grows one thing, started when its words are under way and grown when they are known. It never shrinks.
- The reminder: a notification each day at a time chosen in Settings (18:00 to begin with), unless Today's session is done. A page cannot schedule a notification by itself, so the browser subscribes to Web Push (`lib/reminder.ts`) and Convex keeps the subscription, keyed by its endpoint and needing no account, with the time and time zone (`convex/reminders.ts`). A cron each minute (`convex/crons.ts`) has `convex/push.ts` (a Node action with `web-push`) send those due, skipping a day the page has reported done (`daily/Reminders.tsx`, from any device of a signed-in account), and deletes a subscription the push service says is gone. `public/sw.js` shows it and opens `/#today`; it caches nothing. On an iPhone it works only in the app added to the home screen (iOS 16.4 and later). The keys pushes are signed with are Convex environment variables on both deployments (`VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`); new ones make each browser subscribe again on its next visit.

## First steps

A beginner's course (入门, `/#start`): seven days of three short parts, to take a day at a time, on how Chinese is written and said (characters, tones, pinyin, word order, numbers, character parts) and how Tian shows words, meeting the first words of the bank on the way. The content is `data/course.ts`: a part is screens to read (with things to hear: glyphs, tones, syllables, lines), words to meet and quiz, questions of four, rounds by ear (the tone drills, the pinyin chart's contrasts, numbers) and lines to build. Its lines are the trip's, or its own (`OWN_LINES`, read by Kore), and `courseSpeech` (`lib/course.ts`) lists all it plays for `data/extra.ts`. The way through is kept in `bank.scenes` (`start:1a` is when a part was done), so it follows the player and needs no change to the bank's shape. The course goes on from the part done last, so a player can skip ahead without being sent back to the parts left behind (they come round at the end), and a day taken whole leaves the next for tomorrow, without locking it. It is offered to a beginner until it is done or hidden (`start-hidden` and `start-shown`, the later wins), and while it is, Today's session is its day: what is left of the day's parts, after a review, in place of new words, the trip or shoring up, and listening. Each part is a step (`course`, opened on the course's page, done once `start:<id>` is set, wherever it was played); a day of the course done leaves a usual session for the rest of that day, and hiding the course skips its steps left. So the home screen has one call to action: Today's card leads with the course's day, with the seven days beneath it (`daily/CourseStrip.tsx`) and a cross for someone who knows the basics. The course stays under Skills, and finishing it earns the seal 门.

## Words and text

- `data/words.ts`: the vocabulary, in lessons that follow the trip, then lessons for the games and stories (space, myth and history, ideas, games and music, the starship, the garden, legends, the road west, the market, the town, the quiz, the office), the reader's, and last the topic lessons (software, philosophy, history, myths, astronomy, food, shops, transport, staying well). A row is `hanzi|pinyin|gloss|meaning|tags`. Every id is in one lesson only (a test checks). Pinyin is one syllable per character, dictionary tones; `lib/pinyin.ts` applies the tone changes of 不 and 一 in context (`inContext`), and `lib/numbers.ts` those of 一 in numbers. The gloss is what stands in for the word in mixed text, so it must read well in Chinese order.
- Topics (`data/topics.ts`, `games/topics.tsx`, `/#topics`): what the player wants to learn about, as many as they like, each at a priority from 1 to 3 (Off, 1, 2, 3 on its card; `bank.topics.on`: the ids in the order chosen with their priority, and when; in a merge the later choice wins whole). A topic is one or more lessons, with words from other lessons it needs (`also`, met first); travel in China (food, shopping, transport, hotels and health) is made mostly of the trip's lessons. Today's new words and Learn take the unmet words of the topics chosen first, in rounds of as many from each as its priority, then the rest in lesson order (`toMeet` in `lib/bank.ts`, which `unmet` follows), and today's new words, if not yet begun on, are taken again when the choice changes (`retopic` in `lib/today.ts`). The home screen's Today card names the topics chosen. Hola's themes work the same way; keep the two alike.
- Lines of Chinese anywhere (trip, starship, stall, stories) are written word by word with spaces: `请 给 我=me 你 的 护照 。`. `word=gloss` gives one use a better gloss, `{home}` is the player's country, numbers are one word (`五百一十五`, `八零六` for digits). `lib/text.ts` turns a line into tokens. Every word must be in the bank: `tests/app.test.ts` checks every line of every game.
- The reader (`games/read.tsx`, `/#read`, a text at `/#read/<id>`) has short texts in hanzi (`data/reader.ts`, `data/texts/`), modelled on Hola's Biblioteca: each word a column with its pinyin over it until it is known and its English under it until its meaning holds (its stage, above) (brown while not met, jade while learnt), each row kept on every word so the lines stay even. A tap opens the word's sheet (hear it, Learn it, I know this, and a Learning/Known switch on each of its three links, `setMemory` in `lib/bank.ts`), with a note on the word there where the line has `word#key` and the text a note by that key. A cloud-head divider sets the text off from what it is, the music is silent while it is open (`hushMusic` in `lib/music.ts`), and the mouse pointer hides on a tap on the page or a page turned or scrolled, until the mouse moves. «Aa» has the size, the width, scrolling or pages turned sideways (a swipe, the arrow keys, a tap in the margin; the default, with the narrow width and the second size, as in Hola), the pinyin and the English each None, Until known or All, and the colours: Auto, Light or Dark. Dark is the reader's own: it sets `zh-dark` on the body while the reader is open, which turns the page's colours (`games/read.css`), the word's sheet included. The place read to is kept in the bank (`bank.place`, the later of two in a merge), and «Finished» counts the words met and not looked up as read. The first texts are written for a learner who has taken the trip to Xi'an, in its words; the fables after them are old ones retold simply. The words a text needs beyond the bank are the last lesson, Reading, so that they are met in the reader before Learn comes to them; a test checks every word of the texts is in the bank, every note is there, and every word of Reading is used.
- English with words in it (the quest) writes a word as `{id}`, or `{id=gloss}` for a better gloss in that place.
- `data/syllables.ts` is every Mandarin syllable with a common character for each tone it is said in, written by `uv run scripts/syllables.py` from pypinyin's readings and wordfreq's counts (a character's other readings count only when a common word has them). The pinyin chart (`data/pinyin.ts`) builds its table and its ear training on it. A syllable is spoken in the tones a word has (in others the voice drifts to one that has a word), from a character with no other reading (`READ_AS`; a character with two is read alone as the voice likes best: 撇 asked for as piē came out piě), or one picked by hand where the voice was heard to miss (`READ_INSTEAD` in the script: the rare 熘 came out liú, like the 留 in it), or else from its pinyin, in lists of eight; either way the direction spells out its pinyin and tone, and asks for the puff of air of p, t, k, q, ch and c, which the voice drops from bare pinyin.

## Sound

Speech is made ahead with Gemini TTS (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`). `node scripts/speech.ts > .cache/speech.json` lists everything any game can say (`lib/speech.ts`, with each game's extra lines in `data/extra.ts`, and every number in a line, which can be tapped and heard on its own) and the cast, and `uv run scripts/audio.py .cache/speech.json --job` makes what is missing in Gemini's batch mode, at half the price and outside the daily cap on requests (about 100 on the paid tier), answered within minutes or hours: lines one to a request, words and syllables in lists of eight with a long pause after each, as a teacher reads a word list (a character asked for on its own comes out in a less exact tone). A list that does not cut cleanly is asked for again in halves. `--remake <names>` (or `words`, `lines`, `syllables`) makes them again after a change in the cast, and `--retrim` trims again from the recordings kept. A piece is cut where the sound is quieter than −38 dB, then widened to its quiet edges (−55 dB): cut without the puff of air of p, t and k, tà sounds like dà. Files go to `public/audio/<hash>.mp3` and `data/audio.json` maps `voice|text` to them; files no longer named are removed. A word of the bank is asked for with its pinyin spelt out, since a character on its own can come out in another reading or with an r it does not have (哪 as nǎr). `uv run scripts/hear.py .cache/speech.json` has Gemini listen to the single characters (`--all` for every word, `--only 哪,那`) and names those heard as another syllable or tone, twice over, to listen to; `audio.py --remake 哪,那 --job` makes words again by their text.

The cast is `data/voices.ts`: every voice name the data uses, with the Gemini voice behind it and a few words on how it speaks. Words are read in Kore, as a teacher reads a word list; the trip's people keep the prebuilt voice they are named by, each with a manner of their own; each demon on the road west has a voice and manner of its own; each passenger in the taxi says every line of `data/taxi.ts`; the starship's engineer and everyone it meets on the way (`data/encounters.ts`) have a voice each, the player's replies there in the trip's voice for them; the quiz's host reads its questions; the office's colleagues and its client each have a voice, the player's replies there in the trip's voice for them; and the masters of the sayings, the storyteller, the radio announcer of the slogans, the demon king and the monkey are voices designed from a description (`DESIGNED`, their ids in `scripts/voices.json`: delete one to design it again).

`node scripts/tones-check.ts` compares each word's recorded pitch with its tones and lists the worst, to listen to first. Its ideal contours are no judge of a second tone against a third (it fits most second tones to the third), and nor is Gemini asked by ear: it hears a level first tone as a second and a rising second as a third. Where they disagree, compare the clip with the same voice's other syllables, by where its pitch starts, dips and ends.

Where there is no file, `lib/audio.ts` falls back on the browser's own Chinese voice, so everything works before the sound is made; `tests/app.test.ts` fails while anything on the list has none. A game that plays a line must play it in the voice the list names for it (a line of the trip in its speaker's voice), or it gets the browser's voice.

Everything else is made on the spot with Web Audio: the interface's small sounds (`lib/audio.ts`), music to learn by (`lib/music.ts`, a zither in the five-note scale, off until the player turns it on, quieter while a word is spoken), the fights (`quest/sfx.ts`) and the theatre's clapper (`paper/clapper.ts`).

## The chat

Chat (聊天, `games/chat.tsx`, `/#chat`): a conversation out loud with 林老师, played by Gemini Live (`gemini-3.8-live`, voice Kore) on the player's own API key (bring your own key: pasted on the page, kept in `localStorage` under `tian:gemini:v1` and nowhere else, never in the bank or on the account; `/privacy` says so). The browser talks to Google directly by WebSocket (`lib/live.ts`): the microphone goes up as 16 kHz PCM from an AudioWorklet, the voice comes back as 24 kHz PCM, played as it comes and kept per turn to hear again, with a transcript of both sides. Without headphones the microphone sends silence while the partner speaks, so it does not hear itself. The instructions (`instructions` in `lib/chat.ts`) give the scene chosen (`SCENES`), the pace (slowly or naturally: speed is asked for in words, there is no setting for it), the player's country and topics, and the words of the bank by stage, with the next twenty to meet; a call taken up again (sessions end after about 15 minutes) has the lines so far. Each time it speaks, the partner calls the tool `show_line` with its line word by word (pinyin, English), what it heard the player say, and, after a mistake, how to say it better. `fromItems` shows a word of the bank from the bank (a phrase given as one, as its words) and any other in blue with a dotted line, outside the bank (`outside` on the token: nothing to practise it with, its sheet says so); until a call comes, the transcript is split into the bank's longest words (`segment`). Nothing is recorded in the bank but what a word's sheet records. `?chat=demo#chat` shows a conversation made up, without a key. To try the page without Google, point the WebSocket at a fake server from the test browser (a page script that replaces `window.WebSocket`); the address itself is fixed in the code, so no link can send a player's key elsewhere.

## The podcast

天天: Mandarin with Tian, two hosts (Lin, a teacher, and Erik, a learner) and the trip's people, in English and simple Mandarin, about ten minutes an episode, made from words the games have met. `/#podcast` (`games/podcast.tsx`) plays the episodes from the feed and has buttons that open Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts and Overcast, and the feed's address to copy.

- An episode's script is `podcast/<nn>.json`: its cast (a Gemini prebuilt voice, a designed voice of the game's, or one designed for it) and its lines in order, with chimes and pauses between the parts. `uv run scripts/podcast.py podcast/01.json .cache/podcast/episode-1.mp3` speaks it with Gemini TTS, two voices at most to a request and about a minute each, and joins the pieces; the raw sound of each is kept in `.cache/podcast/`, so a changed line is asked for again alone. The MP3 is listed in `podcast/episodes.json` with its title, description and file. The two hosts are designed voices, `Lin` and `Erik` in `scripts/voices.json`. The words come from the learner's bank, so an episode's Chinese is what they have met, with a few new words said to be new.
- `uv run scripts/podcast-feed.py` tags each MP3 with its title and the cover, writes the feed and uploads it all with Wrangler to the R2 bucket `tian-podcast`, which the Worker serves at `/podcast/` with byte ranges, as podcast apps need: the feed is https://tian.ymse.land/podcast/feed.xml. `--dry-run` builds in `.cache/podcast/out` only. When an episode's sound or the cover changes, it gets a new ID and file name and the old one is deleted, as apps keep what they have for an ID they know; the date each ID went out is in `podcast/published.json`.
- The cover is `podcast/cover.jpg`, the seal over the podcast's name, drawn by `node scripts/icons.mjs --podcast`.
- The feed is marked to stay out of podcast directories (`itunes:block`); anyone with its address can follow it.

## Art

No emoji anywhere in the page's graphics. Plain controls use lucide icons.

- Painted pictures are generated with `node scripts/image.mjs --out <png> --pixel 320x180 --colors 48 "<description>"`, which asks the image tool of the Codex CLI on the ChatGPT plan, then scales the picture down and cuts its palette so it is true pixel art, shown crisp and scaled up. They are in `public/art/`: `shell/` (the home screen's scene at dawn, day, dusk and night), `trip/` (a scene each), `games/` (the rain's town, the stall and its customers), `market/` (a stall per ware, seen across its counter, the trip's `sh-market` as the reference for style), `quest/` (a battlefield per chapter, the fighters and the map of the road west) and `aliens/` (those the starship meets, as on its viewscreen: not pixel art but pictures that look like photographs of stop-motion puppets, as if the original Star Trek series had been made in stop motion, in coloured gels; 600×400 WebP, without `--pixel`). Keep the full-size originals out of `public/`.
- The pictographs (`PICTURES` in `data/parts.ts`) are painted instead, in naturalistic ink and colour, each laid out in the shape of its character where that keeps it natural (月 is a window whose curtain and frame make 月, with the moon outside; a woman or water that would look forced is painted plainly), by `node scripts/pictures.mjs`, with three of the paintings as references for the style; `--web` saves them small to `public/art/pictures/`. First steps shows twelve of them, and the script paints only those unless named.
- What moves is drawn over a picture in its own pixels, as a function of time: `pixel/ambient.ts` (water, lanterns, mist, birds, people passing) and `pixel/props.ts` (a panda, a taxi, fireworks). The trip's scenes are `pixel/art.ts` (picture and layers per scene) on the stage of `pixel/stage.ts`, with people head to toe from `pixel/figure.ts`; its map is `pixel/MapView.tsx`. The home screen's scene is `ornament/scene.ts`, by the player's clock, season and moon.
- Sprites (`pixel/sprites.ts`, rows of letters coloured from a shared palette, some animated) stand in for icons, food, pictographs, demons and the starship, shown with `<Pix name>`; people of the trip, the stall and the bridge are portraits drawn from a few traits (`Look`: hair, hat, beard, glasses, coat) with `<Face look>` (`pixel/portraits.ts`), which blink and talk. `/#gallery` (not on the home screen) shows them all. `tests/app.test.ts` checks that every sprite is well formed and every one the data names exists.
- `ornament/` has the page's decoration: seals, cloud and fret borders, paper-cut corners, lanterns, a brush underline and a celebration.
- The stories are a shadow theatre (皮影戏, `paper/`): jointed leather puppets built from traits (`puppets.ts`), their poses (`acts.ts`), scenery (`scenery.tsx`), a script that says where everything is at each line and beat (`stage.ts`), and a show that moves them with CSS transforms (`show.ts`, `Theatre.tsx`). The covers are the same figures cut from red paper (`PaperCut.tsx`, `still.ts`, `leather.tsx`), with window flowers (`WindowFlower.tsx`).
- The quest's fights are a canvas of their own (`quest/stage.ts`) with its own icons, pixel font and walking party (`quest/icons.ts`, `font.ts`, `walkers.ts`); the starship is 3D with three.js (`games/bridge/`): models shaded smoothly like painted studio miniatures, the ships it meets in `visitors.ts`, under a painted sky (`public/art/space/`, two 2:1 paintings of starfields wrapped round the scene), as if on film (`film.ts`: a glow, a warm grade and grain); the garden draws with the small kit of `pixel/draw.ts`; the taxi's town is drawn once from above and turned under the taxi, so it always drives up the screen (`games/taxiScene.ts`), with the places' signs in the page over it.
